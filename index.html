How to Use

Check things off. Tap a row or its box to mark a load item done. Tap a section header to open or close it. Use “Check All” to clear a whole section at once. Notes can be typed under each section.

Templates are pre-built load lists for a job type (e.g. Large Commercial). Open Templates to build one, then “Use” it to load that exact checklist for today’s job.

Shared links. When the boss sends you a checklist link, opening it asks to load that job’s checklist on your phone. Tap OK and you’re ready to load the truck.
